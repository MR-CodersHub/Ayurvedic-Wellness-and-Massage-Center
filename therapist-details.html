Her story

A healer guided by listening.

Ananya began her study of Ayurveda in the old family tradition of Thrissur, learning classical Abhyanga from her grandmother before formal training at the Kerala Ayurveda Academy. Fourteen years later, her hands remain as attentive as ever.

She is known for an extraordinary sensitivity to tension — finding places the body has learned to hide. Her sessions are slow, deliberate and quietly transformative. She believes the best therapy is the one that leaves you feeling more at home in your own skin.

  • Certified Senior Ayurvedic Therapist, Kerala Ayurveda Academy
  • Advanced Marma therapy training, Chennai lineage
  • Panchakarma specialist with hospital-based experience
