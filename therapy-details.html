The therapy

More than a massage.

In Sanskrit, abhyanga means “oil massage.” But this ritual is far more than the word suggests. Warm herb-infused oil is applied with long, rhythmic strokes that move with the body’s natural flow — soothing the nervous system while renewing the skin and tissues.

Your therapist adjusts pressure, oil and rhythm to your constitution. The result is a session that feels both deeply relaxing and remarkably revitalizing.

The process

Every moment, considered

A calm, unhurried sequence designed for deep restoration.

01

Consultation & assessment

We begin with a conversation about your constitution, tension patterns and the outcome you want from the session.

02

Oil selection

Your therapist warms a freshly blended oil chosen for your dosha — sesame, coconut, almond or a herbal infusion.

03

The full-body ritual

Long, synchronized strokes move from head to toe, with focused attention on joints, marma points and areas of holding.

04

Rest & integration

You are given quiet time to rest, followed by herbal tea and simple aftercare guidance to extend the benefits.

The ingredients

Oils blended with intention.

We press and infuse our oils in small batches. Nothing is mass-produced, and nothing is chosen without regard for your constitution.
