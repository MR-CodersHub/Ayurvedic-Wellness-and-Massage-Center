Featured ritual

The Golden Hour: Abhyanga & Shirodhara

Two of Ayurveda’s most revered therapies woven into one extended ceremony. Warm oil is worked through the body, then poured in a steady thread across the forehead — a journey from physical release to profound mental quiet.

01

Warm preparation

Herbal oil is warmed and applied with long, rhythmic strokes.

02

Marma activation

Pressure points are gently worked to release stored tension.

03

Shirodhara flow

A continuous oil stream invites the mind into deep stillness.
