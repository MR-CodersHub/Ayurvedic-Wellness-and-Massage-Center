Our story

Rooted in wisdom, made for today

For nearly three decades, we have held a simple belief: that healing begins when we are truly listened to.

1998Our first treatment room opened
How it began

A small room, a large intention.

What began as a single treatment room in a heritage home has grown into a sanctuary for thousands — yet the intention remains unchanged. We create space for people to feel heard, held and gently restored.

Our founders learned these rituals at the feet of Kerala’s traditional physicians. Today, that lineage lives on in every oil we blend, every stroke we offer and every silence we keep.

The therapies we offer
What we stand for

Four promises, kept daily

Authenticity

We preserve classical methods rather than dilute them for convenience.

Compassion

Care is offered with warmth, never as a transaction.

Integrity

Honest guidance, transparent pricing and no unnecessary treatments.

Reverence

We treat each guest’s body as a temple — with patience and deep respect.

Our heritage

Where tradition meets thoughtful design.

Our spaces are built from lime-washed walls, reclaimed teak and handwoven linen. Natural light, earthy textures and the faint fragrance of herbs make the building itself part of the therapy.

  • Herbs sourced from small growers in the Western Ghats
  • Oils blended by our in-house herbalist each week
  • Architecture inspired by traditional Kerala homes
